# SECOP tenders plugin

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The requesting user and their team search Colombian public tenders, follow selected
opportunities and prepare applications using their company's supporting evidence.

## Product Purpose

Make a tender's complete available document inventory understandable, answer
questions grounded in the dossier and identify what the company lacks to apply.

## Operating Context

The existing SECOP plugin in shared Savia Preview; desktop and mobile browsers.
Documents come from the SECOP MCP service. Company evidence belongs to a dedicated
private workspace connection. Captcha-blocked downloads require human help.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The approved assistant design lives in the private secop-live-worker repository.
SECOP-specific processing stays there. Savia uses its existing plugin and connector
contracts. Unknown evidence cannot become confirmed compliance. Formal submission
is outside this release. Existing searches, interests, alerts and reports remain.

## Product Principles

- Ground conclusions in source documents and expose incomplete coverage.
- Preserve the user's evidence and progress across sessions.
- Turn missing requirements into actionable preparation tasks.
- Keep human review distinguishable from automated interpretation.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing SECOP process records, document links and the current plugin interface.
The user has not yet supplied company evidence for this assistant. Do not invent
company experience, financial facts or eligibility.
